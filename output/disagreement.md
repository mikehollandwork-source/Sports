# When the money and the tickets disagree

_The games gate 1 throws out. The handle is on one side, the ticket majority on the other, and the board never looks at them again._

- disagreeing games: **113**
- agreeing games, for comparison: **777**

## Who wins them?

| population | backing the MONEY side | backing the TICKET side |
|---|---|---|
| handle vs tickets **disagree** | 54-59 · **-6.2%** (n=113) | 59-54 · **-0.3%** (n=113) |
| handle and tickets agree (the rule's pool) | 459-318 · **+0.6%** (n=777) | — |

_`consensus.py` recorded this population at 12-18 (40%), -23.5% on 30 games when the rule was written. This is the same question with the sample it now has._

## Split by whether the book backs the money

_The cell the question names is the middle row: money one way, tickets the other, and the book refusing to confirm the money — the most disagreement a game can carry._

| order book | backing the MONEY side | backing the TICKET side |
|---|---|---|
| book CONFIRMS the money | 22-19 · **+3.4%** (n=41) | 19-22 · **-10.7%** (n=41) |
| book does NOT confirm the money | 26-28 · **-3.6%** (n=54) | 28-26 · **-1.4%** (n=54) |
| book unreadable | 6-12 · **-35.9%** (n=18) | 12-6 · **+26.5%** (n=18) |

## Does it matter how lopsided the money is?

| handle share (median 64%) | backing the MONEY side |
|---|---|
| handle ≥ 64% | 26-30 · **-11.9%** (n=56) |
| handle < 64% | 26-25 · **+4.2%** (n=51) |

**Does not clear.**

- split-half of **handle < 64% · money**: 19-10 · **+35.6%** (n=29) against 7-15 · **-37.2%** (n=22)

## How to read this

- these games are REJECTED today, so a positive cell would mean volume the rule is leaving behind, not a change to what it backs
- 133 games split three ways is small, and the intervals are wide; the correction is there because three splits of a small population is exactly where a tempting number appears
- nothing here changes the board.
