## Reading it

- a signal is only worth anything if its BACK column beats the vig AND it survives the correction AND both halves agree
- the `sum` column is the fade test: only a signal summing well below the median is wrong by more than the vig
- **raw against park-neutral** is a test of `park_factors.py`: if neutralising is worth anything the neutral row beats the raw one
- **each crowd source alone** is the test the blend cannot do — a source that carries signal and one that cancels it look identical once averaged together
