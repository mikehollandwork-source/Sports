## Gate definitions

1. **handle=tickets** — the dollars are on the same side as the ticket majority
2. **majority** — a ticket majority was read at all
3. **price** — a moneyline exists for the side the consensus names
4. **book read** — ≥2 Polymarket quotes with spread ≤0.15
5. **book confirms** — mid drifted up on that side or resting bid lean >0.2
6. **line against** — implied probability moved AWAY from that side by ≥1.0% (the price discount)
