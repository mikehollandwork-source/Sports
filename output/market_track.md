# Tracking the totals, first five and strikeout lines

_LOGGING AND SETTLEMENT ONLY. Nothing is staked, no price is compared to a payout, and no pick changes. A hit rate is not an edge: a 55% side at -115 is break-even and the vig on a two-sided total is around 4.5%, so nothing here is bettable until a side clears its price by more than its own interval._

_Every reading is the last poll strictly before first pitch minus `consensus.LOCK_LEAD` (15 min). The poller runs three times a day, so an afternoon game is already in progress by the later polls and its line has seen the score._
