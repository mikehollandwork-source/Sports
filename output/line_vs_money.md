## 7. Rigour on the strongest cut (n≥30)

**line DISAGREES with handle — bet the opposite** — 34-21 (62%) · +9.5u · **+17.3%** (n=55)

- Day-block bootstrap 95% CI: **-10.7% to +44.0%** (median +17.3%) — cannot rule out zero.

- Market-calibrated null p-value: **0.043** — this good or better happens 4.3% of the time with realistic prices and no edge.  Significant at 5%.

_Roughly 20 configurations are scanned here. Expect one or two to look great by chance - the holdout column and the p-value are what separate a tell from a coincidence._
